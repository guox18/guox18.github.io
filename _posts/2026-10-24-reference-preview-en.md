This companion test article demonstrates links and navigation between two articles.

## Add background where it helps

When another article already explains a concept, link to it and briefly explain why it is worth reading. Readers can stay with the current text or follow the link to learn more.

For example, the [blog style preview](/blog/2026/style-preview/en/) demonstrates the layout of body text, code, and tables. This article adds a reference example.

## Links and the article map

Links in the text always work. The article map below is an optional way to explore: expand it to see articles this page references and articles that reference this page, then select a node to continue reading.

Chinese and English versions show their own reading paths, so that two translations of the same article do not appear as two different topics.
